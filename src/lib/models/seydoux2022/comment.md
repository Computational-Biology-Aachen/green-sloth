The implementation preserves the main dynamic variables, reaction rates, algebraic PSII states, and fluorescence calculations. The model reproduces the general qualitative behaviour reported in the publication, including NPQ induction and relaxation under changing light conditions and the differences between WT, KEA3 mutant, and KEA3 overexpressor. However, the simulated results do not exactly match the quantitative results presented in the original publication. Differences remain in the NPQ values and kinetic responses. Therefore, the current implementation should be considered a qualitative reproduction rather than an exact quantitative validation of the published results.
