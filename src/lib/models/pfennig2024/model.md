The [Pfennig 2024](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012445) model is a wavelength-dependent kinetic model of photosynthesis in the cyanobacterium _Synechocystis_ sp. PCC 6803. Unlike most photosynthesis models, which take light as a single photon flux density, it resolves the light spectrum: the light absorbed by photosystem I, photosystem II and the phycobilisomes is computed from the spectrum of the light source and the wavelength-dependent absorption of each pigment (chlorophyll a, β-carotene, allophycocyanin and phycocyanin). This makes it possible to ask how the colour of the light, not just its intensity, shapes electron transport, carbon fixation and photoprotection.

Around this light-harvesting description the model represents the photosynthetic and respiratory electron transport chains that share the thylakoid membrane of cyanobacteria: PSII (with state-transition-like quenching) and PSI in quasi-steady state, the plastoquinone pool, cytochrome b6f, plastocyanin, ferredoxin, FNR, the NDH-1 and NDH-2 dehydrogenases, succinate dehydrogenase, the terminal oxidases (Cyd and COX) and flavodiiron proteins. It also covers ATP synthase, lumped Calvin–Benson–Bassham and photorespiratory reactions, a carbon-concentrating mechanism, and orange carotenoid protein (OCP) mediated quenching of the phycobilisomes.
