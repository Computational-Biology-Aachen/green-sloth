**Paper claim, qualitatively true.** The text calls the modelled response of gm (Fig. 7b) "similar to that presented in Fig. 5". Normalised to 25 °C, the model rises from 0.68 at 15 °C to 1.36 at 40 °C (mean slope 0.028 °C⁻¹), while the measured regression rises from 0.51 to 1.78 (0.051 °C⁻¹). Both rise and are nearly linear, so the shapes are similar, but the modelled slope is about half the measured one. Fig. 7 itself is reproduced exactly, so this is a statement about the paper, not an offset in the model.

**Units.** gliq = ρHD/l is per chloroplast surface area; the model multiplies it by Sc for a leaf-area basis, as the text does to get 0.96 mol m⁻² s⁻¹ bar⁻¹. `l` is in µm, so Eqn 14 divides by l × 10⁻⁶. ρH is in mol m⁻³ bar⁻¹ and D in m² s⁻¹. E is stored as 50 000 J mol⁻¹ to match R in J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹.

**No closed-form or fitted parameters.** Every quantity is an explicit function of temperature, and every constant Fig. 7 needs is printed.
