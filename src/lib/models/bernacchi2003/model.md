The [Bernacchi 2003](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.0016-8025.2003.01050.x) model completes the in vivo temperature parameterisation of the Farquhar, von Caemmerer & Berry (1980) model that [Bernacchi 2001](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-3040.2001.00668.x) started for Rubisco-limited photosynthesis, now for RuBP-limited photosynthesis. Electron transport $J$ is the non-rectangular hyperbola of absorbed light (Eqns 2 and 3), and all three of its parameters respond to leaf temperature: the maximum rate $J_{max}$ (an Arrhenius function, Eqn 9), the light-adapted quantum yield of PSII $\Phi_{PSII,max}$ and the convexity $\Theta_{PSII}$ (quadratics, Table 2). These were measured by combined gas exchange and chlorophyll fluorescence at 10–40 °C. The model replaces the usual constant $\Phi_{PSII,max} = 0.85$ and $\Theta = 0.7$, and also gives the response of $J_{max}$ and $\Theta_{PSII}$ to growth temperature (Eqns 12 and 13).

In GreenSloth the leaf model of Appendix 2A is implemented for tobacco (_Nicotiana tabacum_), with $\Gamma^\ast$, $K_c$, $K_o$, $V\_{c,max}$ and $R_d$ from Bernacchi 2001, so $A = (1 - \Gamma^\ast/C_i)\min\{W_c, W_j\} - R_d$. The outputs ending in `_acc` follow Appendix 2B, with $J_{max}$ and $\Theta_{PSII}$ acclimated to the growth temperature $T_g$. The per-growth-temperature fits of Tables 1 and 2 behind Figs 2–4 are also outputs.
