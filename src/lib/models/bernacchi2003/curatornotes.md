**Misprints and inconsistencies.**

- Eqn 11 prints `exp(0.5 (…))`. The other Gaussians (Eqns 12 and 13, Appendix 2B) have `−0.5`, and only `−0.5` gives 1.00 at Tg = Tl = 25 °C. Eqn 11 is not used in the model.
- Eqn 13 (ΘPSII against Tg and Tl, used by Appendix 2B) does not describe Table 2 or Fig. 4c. It gives 0.83 at Tg = Tl = 25 °C (Table 2: 0.98) and 0.40 at Tg 35 °C, Tl 10 °C (Table 2 and Fig. 4c: 0.74). It is up to 0.47 off Table 2 over 10–40 °C. A Gaussian 3-D fit to Table 2 gives k 0.98, mg 25.2, sg 26.0, ml 27.0, sl 34.0, and still misses it by up to 0.08. `Theta_acc` follows Eqn 13 as printed, so `J_acc` and `A_acc` at moderate light should be read with that in mind. `Jmax_acc` (Eqn 12) is consistent: 1.00 at Tg = Tl = 25 °C. At other growth temperatures it is not 1 at Tl = 25 °C (0.79 at Tg 14 °C), as printed. Eqn 12 is the fit to the gas-exchange Jmax,c, whereas Appendix 2A's Jmax,T and the Jmax25 default are the fluorescence Jmax,f. That is how the appendix combines them; Fig. 1 puts Jmax,c at 0.99 Jmax,f for 25 and 35 °C-grown plants, but at 0.88 for 14 °C-grown ones.
- Appendix 2A labels Table 2's light-adapted ΦPSII,max,l "ΦPSII,max". It is the light-adapted value (0.352 + 0.022 Tl − 3.4 × 10⁻⁴ Tl²), which the Discussion argues should be used. Appendix 2A's Jmax,T has ΔHa 43.54 where Table 1 prints 43.5.
- The Appendix 1 symbol list gives β in "%", but Eqn 5 uses 0.5.

**Model.** Appendix 2A: A = (1 − Γ\*/Ci) min{Wc, Wj} − Rd, with Wc = Vc,max Ci / (Ci + Kc (1 + O/Ko)) and Wj = J Ci / (4.5 Ci + 10.5 Γ\*). J follows Eqn 2 with Q2 = Q αl ΦPSII,max β (Eqn 3). Γ\*, Kc, Ko, Vc,max and Rd are the Bernacchi et al. (2001) functions printed in Appendix 2A (Ci basis, µmol mol⁻¹ and mmol mol⁻¹). Every function is explicit, so no root has to be chosen. The `_acc` outputs follow Appendix 2B: Jmax,T by Eqn 12 and ΘPSII by Eqn 13 at growth temperature `Tg`, with Φ unchanged.

**Assumed parameters.** The paper gives no reference leaf. Vc,max,25 = 80 and Rd25 = 1 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ are illustrative. Jmax25 = 136 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ is the 25 °C-grown fit to Fig. 2. αl is measured per leaf (Eqn 6) but not printed, and the conventional 0.85 is assumed. β = 0.5 (Eqn 5). O = 210 mmol mol⁻¹. The fits cover leaf temperatures of 10–40 °C and growth temperatures of 14–35 °C, and the sliders are limited to those ranges.
