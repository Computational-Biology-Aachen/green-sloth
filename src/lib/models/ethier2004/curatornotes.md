Figs 2, 3, 5, 8 and 9 are reproduced. Fig 1 is a schematic without values and Fig 7 is a literature survey of measured data. Figs 4 and 6 plot the Vcmax, Jmax and Km(CO₂)i estimates of least-squares regressions on synthetic A–Ci curves, which the validation language cannot express; the model computes the curves those regressions are fitted to (Fig 5).

**Fig 5** reproduces the ideal A–Ci curves exactly (filled and open symbols: Eqn 10 at gi = 0.2 and 0.05 with the parameters of the Fig 4 legend). The fitted curves are drawn with the printed Vcmax and Jmax and with Kc(1 + O/Ko) and Γ* overestimated by 20 and 10 %. The paper does not print the fitted Rd, nor the gi of the Eqn 10 fits; these were fitted to the ideal points with the printed values held (Eqn 14: Rd = 0.18 and 0.156; Eqn 15: Rd = 0.606 and 0.705; Eqn 10, Rubisco: Rd = 0.088 and 0.048, gi = 0.245 and 0.052; Eqn 10, RuBP: Rd = 0.594 and 0.595, gi = 0.301 and 0.0535, for gi = 0.2 and 0.05). Re-running the regressions themselves gives Vcmax = 46.6 and 24.5 for Eqn 14 (printed: 46.5 and 24.5) and 59.8 for Eqn 10 at gi = 0.05 (printed: 59.8), but the Jmax values only to 2–3 %: which points and constraints the paper used for those fits is not stated.

**Figs 8 and 9.** The five curves of Fig 8 are separate Eqn 10 fits, whose Γ, Kc(1 + O/Ko) and Vcmax − Rd are the points of Fig 9; these were read off Fig 9 (they are not tabulated) and reproduce Fig 8 to within 0.1 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ at Ci = 1600 µbar. The lines of Fig 9 are Eqn 17 and Kc(1 + O/Ko) with Table 3's values for gi = 0.27. The O₂ levels are converted with 21 % = 200 mbar, as Table 3 states, which also places the points of Fig 9. Eqn 17 with Table 3's values gives Γ = 55.3 µbar at 200 mbar, against the 54.9 µbar printed in Table 3. Jmax is set high enough that only Rubisco limits these leaves.

**Fig 2.** The kinetic constants of Jordan & Ogren (1984) and Bernacchi et al. (2001) are scaled to 22 °C with Table 2's Arrhenius parameters; those of Bernacchi et al. (2002) are given at 22 °C in the legend. O₂ is taken as 210 mbar. Rd is not printed and was fitted to the filled points read off the figure, with the printed Vcmax held (1.47 and 1.44 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹); these two curves match the figure. With its printed Vcmax = 33, the curve for Bernacchi et al. (2002) ends about 1.5 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ (6 %) below the drawn one at Ci = 1500 µbar (23.3 against about 24.8), which the printed values do not explain. It is therefore drawn with Vcmax = 36.3 and Rd = 1.68 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹, fitted to the filled points and to the drawn curve read off the figure; the legend keeps the printed 33.

**Fig 3.** Rubisco kinetics of Jordan & Ogren (1984) at 25 °C and 210 mbar O₂; the temperature of the measurements is not stated. Rd is not printed and was fitted to the filled points read off the figure, with the printed Vcmax and gi held. The fitted Rd comes out negative (−0.16 and −0.95 for panel a, −1.07 and −1.68 for panel b, for Eqns 14 and 10). This is inherited from the paper's fits: its Eqn 14 curves cross zero at Ci ≈ 35 and 25 µmol mol⁻¹, below Jordan & Ogren's Γ* of 45 µmol mol⁻¹, which Eqn 14 allows only with Rd < 0. Kinetics at a lower leaf temperature with Rd ≥ 0 match the figure worse, not better. With these values the curves stay within about 4 % of the figure.
