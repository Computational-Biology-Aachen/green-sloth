The authors provide the model as a Mathematica notebook. That notebook includes the model implementation, but not the simulation used for each figure. The reimplementation was based entirely on the notebook, yet errors remain in reproducing the figures. The errors mostly stem from simulations with light oscillations and very short periods. Because of these reproduction errors, some later figures could not be reproduced, also due to missing information on how. However, the steady-state figure is reproduced with very high accuracy; therefore, this model reimplementation is deemed semi-validated, with caution advised. Any support towards a better implementation is encouraged.
