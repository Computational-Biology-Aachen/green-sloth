The O₂ partial pressure is 20 kPa, that of the measurements (Fig. 1, Table 2). With 21 kPa, net assimilation at the operating points of Fig. 8 comes out about 2 % low; with 20 kPa all three printed operating points are reproduced exactly (A = 21.1, 27.4 and 26.3 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ at Ci = 25.9, 49.5 and 48.4 Pa, with stomatal conductances of 370, 283 and 253 mmol m⁻² s⁻¹).

The model uses $C_i$ as its driving variable and solves Eqns 10 and 11 for the ambient CO₂ and stomatal conductance that give it, instead of iterating for $C_i$ at a given ambient CO₂ as the paper does; the two are equivalent. Dark respiration (Fig. 2) is computed from the paper's fits but not used by the model, which holds $R_d$ at 0.82 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ as in the paper.
