The [von Caemmerer, Farquhar & Berry 2009](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4020-9237-4_9) chapter restates the Farquhar, von Caemmerer & Berry (1980) model of C₃ photosynthesis in its current form, by its three authors. Net CO₂ assimilation is the minimum of the Rubisco-limited, the RuBP-regeneration (electron transport) limited and the triose phosphate export limited rates (Eqn 9.21). Compared with the 1980 paper it adds the non-rectangular light response of electron transport (Eqns 9.15–9.17), triose phosphate utilisation (Eqns 9.18, 9.19), a finite internal conductance $g_i$ between the intercellular airspaces and the chloroplasts, which makes the Rubisco- and RuBP-limited rates quadratics in $C_i$ (Eqns 9.28, 9.29), and Arrhenius temperature functions for the Rubisco kinetics, $V_{c,max}$ and $R_d$ (Eqn 9.22), with the peaked function of Farquhar et al. (1980) for $J_{max}$ (Eqns 9.23, 9.24).

GreenSloth evaluates the rates both at the chloroplast ($g_i = \infty$, with $C_i$ standing for the chloroplastic partial pressure, as in the chapter's Fig 9.5) and with a finite $g_i$. The defaults are the tobacco parameters of Table 9.1 at 25 °C and 200 mbar O₂ that the chapter's model figures are drawn with (with $\Gamma^*$ = 36.9 µbar, the table's bracketed value, which those figures use), and $g_i$ = 0.3 mol m⁻² s⁻¹ bar⁻¹ from its Fig 9.8. All CO₂ and O₂ values are partial pressures (µbar, mbar). The stomatal supply function $A = g(C_a - C_i)$ of Fig 9.7 is included as a separate output.
